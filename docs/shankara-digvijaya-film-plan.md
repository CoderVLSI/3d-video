# Madhaviya Shankara Digvijaya — animated film plan

Working title: **శంకర దిగ్విజయం** (*Shankara Digvijaya*)
Format: Telugu-narrated 3D animated film, built with the existing pipeline (ElevenLabs → Three.js frames → ffmpeg).

## 1. The source

- *Madhaviya Shankara Digvijaya* (also *Sankshepa-Shankara-Vijaya*): 16 sargas, about 1,843 verses, a hagiography of Adi Shankara, traditionally attributed to Madhava-Vidyaranya (14th c.).
  Authorship and date are disputed by scholars; the film should present it as the *traditional account* ("as told in the Madhaviya Shankara Digvijaya"), not as verified history.
  Other hagiographies (Anandagiri's, Chidvilasa's) differ on details. Where they differ, **follow the Madhaviya** and note the variant.
- Best sources to work from (all blocked from the cloud container, so the user must supply a copy):
  - Sringeri matha's *abridged English translation of the Madhaviya Shankara Digvijayam* (web: sringeri.net, with a PDF `files.sringeri.net/.../sri-shankara-digvijayam.pdf`; the web version is split into parts with headings such as "Divine Descent", "Initiation and study under Sri Govinda Bhagavatpada", "Sri Shankara and Kumarila Bhatta").
  - The Sanskrit edition on the Internet Archive ("Sri Madhaviya Shrimat Shankara Digvijayah", Kashichit Shankara Kinkara), including a full-text djvu file.
- **Status:** the user supplied the Sringeri abridgment (read in full, 19 pp.). For *complete detail* we still need a full 16-sarga translation (e.g. Swami Tapasyananda's *Sankara-Dig-Vijaya* published by Sri Ramakrishna Math, or the Sanskrit edition with translation); both must be provided by the user.

## 2. Story outline — verified against the Sringeri abridgment (19 pp., read in full)

Source in hand: *Madhaviya Shankara Digvijayam — abridged biography*, Sringeri Sharada Peetham (uploaded by the user).
It is a **condensation** (~9,000 words) that keeps the episodes "remembered to this day". The complete Madhaviya has 16 sargas and
many more incidents; for "complete detail" we also need a full translation (see §1). Tags: **[P]** confirmed in the Sringeri PDF.

Key corrections to my earlier outline (from search summaries): the Kapalika is killed by **Padmapada** possessed by Narasimha,
*not* by Shankara; the "boon of an omniscient but short-lived son" is **not** in this text; the opponent in Kamarupa/Karmapura is **Navagupta**
(a Shakta), not Abhinavagupta; Mandana's town Mahishmati is placed in present-day Bihar by this source.

**Prologue** [P] Krishna's words *yada yada hi dharmasya*. 8th-c. India: Buddhism decayed into ritual pomp; Mimamsa (Jaimini, Kumarila) revived
sacrifice but made ritual the goal. Dakshinamurti (Shiva under the banyan) leaves his silence and walks the earth as Shankaracharya.

1. **Birth** [P] Kalady on the Purna (Poorna) river, Kerala. Shivaguru (Nambudiri, Krishna Yajur Veda) and Aryamba pray at the Vrishachala mound in Trichur (Thrissur). Born 788 A.D., Vaishakha shukla panchami, Arudra star, five planets exalted.
2. **Boyhood** [P] Shivaguru dies while Shankara is a child; Aryamba raises him; upanayana; mastery of the Vedas and Sanskrit very young.
   - *The gold amalaka*: begging alms, a poor woman gives her only dried amalaka; he composes the **Kanakadhara Stotram**; Lakshmi showers golden amalakas. [P]
   - *The river moves*: Aryamba faints returning from her bath; Shankara prays; by morning the Purna has changed course to flow near their home. [P]
3. **The crocodile** [P] In the Purna a crocodile seizes his leg; he asks leave to take sannyasa ("a new birth"); trembling, Aryamba consents; the crocodile lets go. Aryamba consents *only* to sannyasa, not to relatives doing her last rites; Shankara promises (Madhaviya 5.71): "whenever you think of me I will come… I myself will perform your last rites." He leaves her in the relatives' care.
4. **Govinda Bhagavatpada** [P] On the Narmada, in a forest hut with deerskin and bark; Govinda, disciple of Gaudapada (earlier Patanjali) is in samadhi. "Who are you?" — Shankara answers in the *Dasha Shloki* ("only one remains, and that Shiva I am"). Govinda: *sa prahá Shankarah sa Shankara eva sakshat.* Shankara worships his guru's feet; learns the four Mahavakyas and Vyasa's Vedanta Sutras. When the **Narmada floods**, he stills it with *Aum* without disturbing his guru's penance. Govinda sends him to Varanasi to write the Brahma-sutra commentary.
5. **Varanasi and the Chandala** [P] First disciple Sanandana. Walking to the Ganga a Chandala with four dogs approaches; Shankara's disciples tell him to keep away; the Chandala's reply (is the Self different in a Brahmin and an outcaste?) leads to *Manisha Panchakam*. The Chandala vanishes: **Shiva and the four Vedas** appear and command him to write the Brahma-sutra commentary, refute false views, appoint guardians of the Vedic path, then return to Shiva.
6. **The Bhashyas** [P] Badri: writes (in his twelfth year) the commentaries on the Brahma Sutras, Gita and Upanishads (Prasthanatraya); returns to Varanasi; also commentaries on Sanatsujatiya, Nrisimhatapani, Vishnu Sahasranama, Lalita Trishati.
7. **Padmapada** [P] Sanandana on the far bank is called by Shankara; he steps onto the Ganga, which raises a **lotus under each footstep**; named Padmapada.
8. **Refuting the schools** [P] Pashupatas challenge him (Moksha as an event in time would end; if all of Ishvara's qualities enter the jiva, Pashupati becomes the ignorant soul). The Madhaviya's survey of rival schools (Buddhists, Nyaya-Vaisheshika, Kumarila, Samkhya, Patanjala, Charvaka).
9. **Vyasa** [P] An old Brahmin debates the Sutras for **eight days**; Padmapada realises it is Vyasa. Vyasa: Shankara alone has known the meaning. Shankara wishes to give up the body; Vyasa refuses: his life was eight years; Agastya and others' satisfaction added eight; **sixteen more** by Shiva's grace (to age 32).
10. **Kumarila Bhatta** [P] Prayaga. Kumarila stands in a **husk-fire** in expiation for betraying his Buddhist teacher; recognises Shankara; Shankara calls him an incarnation of Skanda and offers to put out the fire; Kumarila declines, asks for Brahma-vidya (given), and directs him to **Mandana Mishra (Vishvarupa)** in Mahishmati.
11. **Mandana Mishra** [P] Shankara enters his house as he washes the feet of **Vyasa and Jaimini**; Mandana (dislikes sannyasins) argues furiously; sages calm him; the loser becomes the other's disciple; **Ubhaya Bharati** (Saraswati) judges; a **garland on each** — whoever's withers loses; debate runs several days; Shankara's proposition (Brahman like the shell mistaken for silver) vs. Mandana's (karma is the only means); Mandana's garland fades; he takes sannyasa; Ubhaya Bharati gives bhiksha to both; Mandana's final questions on Jaimini answered; Mandana prostrates (*Tat tvam asi*).
12. **Ubhaya Bharati's challenge** [P] She must be defeated too; **17 days** of debate; she turns to **Kama Shastra**; he asks a month. **Parakaya-pravesha**: flying with disciples, they find the dead **King Amaruka**; Padmapada consents but cites Matsyendra; Shankara answers; he enters the body, rules justly, learns; the ministers suspect, order **unclaimed bodies burnt**; disciples come as musicians to remind him; he leaves the king, finds his own body already set on fire, chants the **Lakshmi-Narasimha Karavalamba Stotram**; the fire dies; he flies to Mandana's house; Ubhaya Bharati concedes (moon and stars before the sun), promises to depart; Shankara promises temples for her in **Sringeri** and elsewhere; she vanishes. Mandana becomes **Sureshwara**.
13. **The Kapalika** [P] A Kapalika needs the head of a king or an all-knowing man to reach Kailasa bodily; asks Shankara (cites Dadhichi); Shankara agrees on condition of secrecy and his disciples' absence; sits in samadhi; **Padmapada, far away, senses it**, is seized by Narasimha, leaps, tears the Kapalika's chest open; Shankara wakes, sees **Narasimha**, pacifies him with a hymn; notes that the Kapalika attained liberation by dying at the Lord's hands.
14. **Hastamalaka** [P] Gokarna, Hari-Shankara (Harihar: eleven verses to Shiva-Vishnu), **Mookambika**; village Sri Bali: Prabhakara brings his seven-year-old "idiot" son, who answers "Who are you?" in twelve verses (*Hastamalakiyam*, "an amla in the palm"); joins as Hastamalaka.
15. **Sringeri** [P] Rishyasringa's hermitage. Shankara builds a temple "as graceful as Indraloka", installs the Divine Mother and invokes her to stay as **Sharada** (keeping her promise). Disciple **Giri**, thought dull, is awakened and runs to class dancing, reciting the *Totakashtakam*: **Totakacharya**. Four disciples write treatises (Sureshwara's Naishkarmya Siddhi; Padmapada's work on the Brahma-sutra bhashya; Ananda Giri (Totaka) etc.).
16. **Aryamba's last days** [P] He feels it, "transports himself to Kalady in no time"; she cannot absorb the Impersonal Brahman; he recites a hymn on **Shiva** — Shiva's attendants with tridents appear, she is frightened; a hymn on **Vishnu** — she sees the radiant form; she passes away absorbed in the Lord. Neighbours and relatives refuse help ("what right has a sannyasin?"), refuse even fire; **he makes fire from his own right hand** and cremates her; he curses them (unfit to study the Vedas; no sannyasin to come for bhiksha; cremate in their own compounds). He grants his mother **Vaikuntha**.
17. **Panchapadika** [P] Padmapada's pilgrimage north and south; at Srirangam he leaves his manuscript with his uncle; it is lost in a fire; Shankara, in Kerala, comforts him and **dictates from memory** the five chapters he had once heard.
18. **Tour and opponents** [P] Defeats Jains, Madhyamikas, Pashupatas, followers of Bhatta Bhaskara; some resort to violence; **King Sudhanva** and his army put them down. At **Karmapura** he defeats **Navagupta**, a Shakta, who follows him pretending to be a disciple and afflicts him with black magic; the **Ashwini Devas** confirm the cause; Padmapada, furious, transfers the disease back to Navagupta by mantra.
19. **The four Amnaya Mutts** [P] Puri (East, Rig Veda, Hastamalaka), Dwaraka (West, Sama Veda, Padmapada), Sringeri (South, Yajur Veda, Sureshwara), Badrinath (North, Atharva Veda, Totaka). *(Note: the text's own sentence order lists the Vedas as Rig/Yajur/Sama/Atharva for Puri/Sringeri/Dwaraka/Badri; check the original verse when using it on screen.)*
20. **Vision of Gaudapada** [P] On the Ganga at Kashi; Shankara reads his commentaries on the Mandukya Upanishad and Karika; Gaudapada, "deathless and desireless", blesses him and disappears.
21. **Sarvajna Peetham, Kashmir** [P] The four-gated Sharada temple; scholars from W, E and N had opened their doors, none from the South. At the southern gate Nyaya, Samkhya, Buddhist, Digambara Jain and Mimamsa scholars examine him in their own systems and open it. As he is about to ascend (holding Padmapada's hand) **Sharada's voice** says omniscience needs purity too, citing the Amaruka stay; he replies he has done no sin with *this* body; she is silent; he ascends; conches, drums, flowers.
22. **The end** [P] Back to Badri (Patanjali school accepts Advaita); hymns (Shivananda Lahari, Soundarya Lahari, to Ganesha, Subrahmanya, Sharada, Lakshmi, Narasimha…); at age 32 to **Kedara**; Madhaviya's account: Rishis and Devas with Brahma; **Nandi** stands before him; amid hymns and flowers he mounts Nandi, Brahma supporting, and becomes Shiva, returning to Shivaloka. (Tradition: he disappeared at Kedarnath.)
23. **Epilogue** [P] He instituted worship of Ganesha, Shiva, Shakti, Surya, Vishnu, Subrahmanya on one pedestal; his verse "the wave belongs to the ocean, not the ocean to the waves".

Not in this abridgment but in the full Madhaviya / other Digvijayas (to be checked in a full translation before including): Shankara's debates in Kanchipuram, the Narmada's other miracles, Srisailam (the Kapalika's *other* version), many more disciples and temple visits.

## 3. Film design

- **Length:** ~20 minutes in 10 chapters of ~2 min (see §5 for why not longer).
- **Structure:**
  - Act I *The Descent* — ch. 1 Prologue + Kalady and the boon; ch. 2 prodigy and the crocodile; ch. 3 leaving home, Govindapada.
  - Act II *The Debates* — ch. 4 Kashi and Vyasa; ch. 5 Kumarila; ch. 6 Mandana Mishra; ch. 7 Ubhaya Bharati and Amaruka.
  - Act III *The Victory* — ch. 8 the Kapalika and Srisailam; ch. 9 four mathas, Sringeri, Kashmir; ch. 10 Aryamba and the Himalayas.
- **Narration:** Telugu, in the user's mother's cloned voice (sudha, `eleven_v4`, no settings override). **Dialogue** lines for characters in separate ElevenLabs voices (to be chosen by the user from the account's voices; the account has Roger, George, Will, Charlie, Eric, etc., plus clones).
- **Tone:** reverent; show *debate* through staging (two figures, text-less gestures, the garlands, camera cuts), not through subtitles of philosophy; short title cards for place names (Telugu).
- **Music:** 3–4 reusable themes (devotional drone/flute, debate tension, mountain/ethereal, ending), looped; avoid generating one track per chapter.

## 5. Feasibility and cost (measured on this container)

- Rendering is CPU-only: **~1.1 frames/s at 1280×720**. One minute at 30 fps = 1,800 frames ≈ 27 minutes of render.
  - 20-min film at 720p/30 ≈ **9 hours** of continuous render.
  - At 960×540 and 24 fps ≈ **4 hours**.
- The container is reclaimed whenever I stop working, so renders only progress while I am active in a turn (the resumable frame cache protects against restarts). In practice: **about 2 chapters per working session** at 720p.
- A GPU machine would make this 10–50× faster. If the film is meant to be the real thing, rendering on the user's own PC (the repo is plain Node + Chromium + ffmpeg) is worth considering.
- ElevenLabs: Telugu narration ≈ 600 characters per 75 s, so a 20-min film ≈ 10,000 characters; dialogue adds a little; music for 4 themes is the larger unknown (a track cost roughly 1,500 credits earlier). Budget on the order of **15–25k credits**; the account has ~97k.
